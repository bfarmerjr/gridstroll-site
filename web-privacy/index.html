GridStroll Website Privacy Policy

Effective date · November 25, 2026

This policy covers gridstroll.com. The GridStroll iOS app has its own App Privacy Policy.

Website visits

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes. The site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, embedded third-party content, or contact forms.

Email

If you email me, I receive your email address, message, and any information you include. I use that information to respond and handle your request.

Tips

The tips page links to Cash App, Venmo, and PayPal. If you follow a link, that provider handles your visit and any payment under its own privacy policy. This site does not ask for or process payment details.

Contact

For questions about this website’s privacy practices, email bill@gridstroll.com.
