GridStroll Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

GridStroll is designed to work offline. The app does not collect or send personal information, gameplay activity, diagnostics, or device identifiers to the developer or to third parties. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or AI service.

Information stored on your device

GridStroll saves a small amount of app data locally so it can remember your game and preferences:

  • Current and highest unlocked level
  • Best dots collected on each level and lifetime dots collected
  • Whether the short introductions to game mechanics have been shown
  • Haptics and appearance preferences

This information is used only to restore your progress and settings. GridStroll does not upload it, and the developer has no access to it through the app. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

You can erase level, score, badge, and mechanic-introduction progress in the app using Help and settings → Clear all game progress. Haptics and appearance preferences are kept when you clear progress and can be changed in the app. Deleting GridStroll removes its app data from the device. If you use iCloud or computer backups, Apple may include app data in a device backup under your backup settings and Apple's policies; GridStroll cannot access or control those backups.

Network access and external links

GridStroll does not make background network requests or send game data to a server. The app opens an external page only when you choose a link, such as Privacy Policy or the optional tips page. Your browser then connects to that website. The website and its hosting provider may receive standard connection information needed to provide the page, such as your IP address, the requested page, the time of the request, and browser or device information. Their handling of that information is governed by the website's privacy practices. No GridStroll game progress or preferences are attached to these requests.

Sharing

GridStroll does not sell, share, or disclose app data. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or other third-party software that receives app data.

Retention and deletion

On-device game progress and preferences remain on your device until you clear progress, change your preferences, or delete the app. Clearing progress removes the saved levels, scores, lifetime dots, and mechanic-introduction records. The app has no account or server-side copy to delete. Backup copies, if enabled, are managed separately through Apple's device-backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If GridStroll's data practices change, this policy and the app's App Store privacy information will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

For questions about GridStroll’s privacy practices, email bill@gridstroll.com.
