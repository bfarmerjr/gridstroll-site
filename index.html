GridStroll

A calm path puzzle for iPhone

Draw a path from the circle to the diamond without visiting any dot twice. Drag to draw your route or tap one dot at a time. You can undo a move, start over, or take as long as you like. There’s no timer or penalty for changing your route.

Start on a 4 × 4 board and work through 1,000 levels. New moves and obstacles appear as you go, and you can replay completed levels to improve your best score. GridStroll works offline and has no ads or accounts.
