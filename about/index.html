How to Play & Support

Start at the circular marker and connect dots until you reach the diamond-shaped goal. Drag to draw a path or tap dots one at a time. Each dot can be visited once per route. You can undo a move or start over at any time.

Moves and markers

  • Move horizontally or vertically in early levels. Diagonal moves are introduced at level 26, and that level requires a diagonal move.
  • Skip crossed dots. They’re blocked.
  • Visit every circled waypoint before reaching the goal.
  • Blue diamond bonus dots count as two dots.

Levels, stars, and badges

Clear a level to unlock the next one. The level picker shows your unlocked levels, and you can replay any completed level. Boards grow from 4 × 4 to 10 × 10 by level 31. Starting at level 101, changing layouts and more special dots gradually increase the difficulty.

Dots along your route between the start and goal contribute to your score. Clear a level to earn one star, collect at least 50% of its possible dots for two stars, or collect at least 80% for three. Your progress and best scores are saved on your device. Eight decorative badges unlock at lifetime dot milestones. Tap the badge row to show or hide your score stats.

Haptics are optional and off by default.
